Day 53 Learning Summary

✅ Topic Covered
Introduction to Pragmatics (Natural Language Processing / Linguistics)
Difference between Literal Meaning and Pragmatic Meaning
Understanding how the same sentence can have different interpretations depending on context.

✅ Example Studied
sentence = "Can you pass the salt?"
Literal Meaning:
The speaker is asking whether you are capable of passing the salt.

Pragmatic Meaning:
The speaker is making a polite request for you to pass the salt.

✅ Key Concept Learned
Computers interpret language literally, whereas humans understand implied meaning from context.
Pragmatics helps AI and NLP systems infer the speaker's actual intention rather than just the literal words.
